Read this first. This lake is a curated scrape — Tech + Business ≈ 67% of posts, ~79% educational — not a random sample of Instagram. Sub-niche deltas on small samples (n < 30) are indicative, not conclusive.

What this means

  • Talking-head posts outperform on engagement quality — 16.0% standout, the highest, though only 3 pure talking-head accounts feed it (small sample).
  • Carousels and single-image text are near-tied on standout (12.2% vs 11.3%); carousels win on raw reach (median 551 vs 322 likes).
  • Within an account (same audience, same creator), carousels beat that account's other formats on only ~59% of accounts (29 of 49), and the average edge is just +2.9pp — driven by a handful of large winners.

What this means

  • Both strategies saturate: standout rates drift down across 2026 as posting volume climbs and baselines tighten — a whole-corpus effect, not format-specific.
  • The carousel-listicle cohort has tracked at or below the other-faceless cohort for most of the window (Apr–May dip to ~4–7% standout), recovering to ~11% by Aug.
  • Raw median likes for carousel accounts also fell (1,163 → 159) as more accounts entered the space — a classic supply-side squeeze on faceless content.

The pattern: the carousel/listicle format over-indexes in aspirational, list-friendly, self-improvement niches — freelancing, social-media marketing, personal finance, sales, AI-art how-tos. It underperforms in skills/credentials-demanding niches — software engineering, cybersecurity, education, digital marketing. Swipeable listicles win where viewers want quick tips; they lose where audiences expect depth and proof. Caveat: several “winning” niches are dominated by a single star account (e.g. Sales ≈ jackblairofficial alone), so the delta partly reflects creator skill, not just the format.

What separates the winners

The strongest carousel-list accounts (jackblairofficial, girsta) sit on the higher end but still trail the corpus's best faceless all-rounders (tembrasdev 34.5%, mrnotion.co 29%). Notably, the highest raw-likes carousel accounts are not the highest-quality — reach ≠ standout. The format works, but it does not create unfair advantage by itself.

Bottom line for the strategy

Faceless listicle/carousel accounts are a viable volume and reach model in this cohort, best applied to self-improvement and quick-tip niches (finance, freelancing, marketing, sales). They saturate fast and face a supply squeeze. For depth/credibility niches the format is a drag — talking-head or single-image with real substance outperforms. The ceiling is set by the creator, not the carousel.
